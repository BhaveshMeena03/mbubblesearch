What the ticker said, and what happened.

Every Polymarket market Market Bubble ran along the bottom of its broadcast. The odds it showed, the second it was on screen, and how each one came out.

The odds are Polymarket's, shown by the show's sponsor. They are not a call by Ansem or Banks, and none of this is advice. A market that went the way its odds leaned is the market being right, not the hosts.

How a market gets here: the ticker is read off the broadcast itself, a frame every twenty seconds, and the time each frame aired comes from X's own record of the replay, checked against the clock in the corner of the picture. What the ticker said is only ever used to find the market: the question shown here is Polymarket's own, and it has to match every figure and every month of what was on screen. Polymarket's price for that market, at the minute it was on screen, has to match the odds the ticker showed, within two points, or the row is not here. A person then reads every row, and only the ones marked keep are shown. Where a market now stands is checked with Polymarket every fifteen minutes. How the archive is built.
