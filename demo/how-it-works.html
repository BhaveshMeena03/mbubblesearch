How Market Bubble Search works

Six archives of shows and interviews, indexed by meaning, and answered only from what was actually said.

1

Transcribe every recording

More than 2,000 hours across more than 1,500 recordings: Market Bubble, MCG Live, ThreadGuy, rasmr, Elon Musk's interviews and The Record. New ones are added every morning, and a show that went out twice is counted once.

Whisper, and a show's own captions
2

Cut into overlapping passages

Passages of up to 2,400 characters, each sharing its last 240 with the next, so an answer that straddles a boundary is still findable.

every line keeps its timestamp
3

Index by meaning, not keywords

Each passage becomes a 1,024-dimension vector, so "why is eth done" finds it even if nobody said those words.

voyage-3.5 → Pinecone
4

Index the rare words exactly

More than 130,000 rare words, names and numbers matched literally. "54 million" barely moves a vector, but it is the question.

the half meaning-search misses
5

Narrow to what actually answers it

Both routes feed 50 candidates into a reranker, which keeps only the few that genuinely bear on the question.

rerank-2.5-lite
6

Answer from those passages only

A language model writes the answer and cites the line it used: the second you can click, on X or on YouTube.

claude-haiku-4-5

If it wasn't said on the show, it says so.

No outside knowledge. An answer with no citation is never posted. The timestamp is the part you can check.
