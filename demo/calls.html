What they called, and what it did.

Every time Ansem or FaZe Banks said on air that he owned something, named a pick or put a number on it. Quoted word for word, linked to the second he said it, priced from that night to today. The misses are here too.

This is a record of what was said on the show. It is not advice and not a track record of anybody's trading: a host naming a coin says nothing about what he bought, how much, or when he sold. The number on each row is only what the price has done since the broadcast.

How a row gets here: a model reads each live show and proposes calls. Its quote has to be in the transcript word for word, on a line that carries that host's voice label, or it is thrown out. A person then reads every one that is left, and only the ones marked keep are shown. Tokens are priced at the midnight UTC nearest the broadcast and stocks at that day's close, from the exact listing, never from a ticker alone. Guests are not on the board because their voices are not labelled. How the archive is built.
